## The useful question is narrower

An AI assessment can sound impressive while still being weakly grounded. The more jobs it tries to do at once, the harder it becomes to tell whether its conclusions are useful. A broad assessment may produce a polished overview, but polish does not establish that the result reflects a person's real capability gaps.

The narrower opportunity is a gap interview. Start from grounded expectations, identify a possible gap, and let the system drill down with focused questions. The role of AI is not to declare a complete or final judgment. It is to help examine one uncertain area closely enough that a person can decide what to verify next.

## Grounding before breadth

That narrower role changes the standard for a good interaction. Each follow-up should remain connected to the gap being explored. If an answer adds uncertainty, the next question should clarify it rather than opening another topic. If the available grounding cannot support a conclusion, the assessment should say so.

This framing also makes the boundary legible. The system is assisting an interview and a drill-down, not replacing the wider assessment process. Its output is a focused input for further review.

## A next experiment

A useful next step would compare a broad assessment flow with a grounded gap interview. The question is modest: which format produces observations that are easier to verify and act on? That experiment would teach more than adding another layer of confident language to an over-broad result.
